About Glint fast Shoe Repair

Your local shoe repair shop in downtown Washington, DC

Glint fast Shoe Repair provides dependable, high-quality shoe and leather repair for customers throughout Washington, DC. We combine time-tested cobbler techniques with modern materials to deliver clean, durable results.

From weekday professionals to weekend visitors, our team is committed to restoring comfort, extending shoe life, and providing service that is honest, clear, and fast.
